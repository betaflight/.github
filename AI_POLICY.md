# AI Contribution Policy

Betaflight accepts contributions written with the help of AI tools, and does not
ask you to declare that you used one.

This is the canonical AI contribution policy for **all Betaflight
repositories**. Individual repositories may link here rather than duplicating
it.

## The rule

What you submit is yours. Whether you wrote it by hand, an assistant drafted it,
or the two of you worked it out between you makes no difference to how it is
handled: you are the author of record, you answer for it in review, and it is
read to the same standard as every other pull request. Nothing is waved through
because a model wrote it, and nothing is held to a higher bar for the same
reason.

That standard is the one we already apply. You should be able to say what the
change does and why, answer questions on it without going back to a tool for the
answer, and confirm you have read every line and tested it, on hardware where
the change calls for it. The right to submit it under the repository's licence
is yours to confirm as well, not the tool's.

## What we will not accept

Output nobody has read. A patch the author cannot explain moves the work of
understanding it onto the reviewer, and reviewer time is the scarcest thing this
project has. Pull requests that show no sign of human judgement will be closed,
and submitting them repeatedly is handled under the
[Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

AI tools are welcome when they help a contributor do good work. They are not a substitute for understanding that work. If you submit a change, you are expected to have reviewed it, understood it, and taken responsibility for it yourself—not to rely on the tool to do that on your behalf.
